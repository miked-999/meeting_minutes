Meeting Transcribe

Air-Gapped Speech-to-Text & Speaker Diarisation v1.0.0

Tool Overview & Purpose

Meeting Transcribe is an air-gapped, privacy-first meeting transcription application designed to convert audio and video recordings into structured text, Word (.docx) documents, and PDFs 100% locally.

Powered by faster-whisper (CTranslate2) and SpeechBrain ECAPA-TDNN speaker diarization, all processing occurs directly on your CPU without transmitting any data over external networks or third-party APIs.

Simple User Guide

1
Upload Media File

Drag & drop or browse an audio or video file (MP4, MKV, MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, etc.) up to 90+ minutes.

2
Select Options & Diarization

Choose STT model size (small for speed, medium for higher accuracy). Enable Speaker Diarisation to auto-cluster distinct speakers.

3
Track & Stop Progress

Watch real-time transcription segment streaming. Stop or cancel an in-progress job anytime using the Cancel button.

4
View & Export Documents

Download generated transcripts in Word (.docx), PDF, TXT, or SRT Subtitle formats with optional timestamp toggles.

Privacy & Security Notice: Source files are automatically deleted upon processing completion. Jobs are isolated per browser session and automatically purged based on retention policies.
